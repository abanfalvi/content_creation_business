# Adapting Content Structure for Platform Nuances

## When to Use

- Generating multiple posts for the same day across different platforms
- Need to maintain persona consistency while adapting to platform-specific norms
- Working with platforms that have distinct content expectations (e.g., short-form vs. long-form)

## Workflow

### 1. Identify Platform Requirements

For each target platform, determine:
- **Preferred length**: Short, immediate thoughts vs. longer, descriptive narratives
- **Formatting conventions**: Use of hashtags, scene-setting intros, visual cues
- **Tone expectations**: Casual/immediate vs. polished/descriptive

### 2. Maintain Persona Consistency

- Keep the core voice, personality, and messaging consistent across all platforms
- The persona should feel like the same person regardless of the medium
- Only adapt the delivery mechanism, not the fundamental character

### 3. Adapt Structure Per Platform

**For platforms favoring short, immediate content (e.g., Threads):**
- Keep posts concise and direct
- Avoid hashtags unless platform-native
- Skip lengthy scene-setting intros
- Use punctuation that feels conversational and immediate

**For platforms allowing longer narratives (e.g., Instagram):**
- Longer, descriptive content is acceptable
- Hashtags and tags can be included as appropriate
- Scene-setting intros may enhance the post
- Visual cues and formatting can be more elaborate

### 4. Quality Checks

Before finalizing, verify:
- [ ] Does each post feel native to its platform?
- [ ] Is the persona's voice consistent across all versions?
- [ ] Are platform-specific conventions (hashtags, length, formatting) correctly applied?
- [ ] Does the content avoid feeling like a copy-paste across platforms?

## Example Pattern

| Platform | Length | Hashtags | Intros | Visual Cues |
|----------|--------|----------|--------|-------------|
| Threads  | Short  | Minimal  | None   | Minimal     |
| Instagram| Longer | Yes      | Optional | More elaborate |

## Key Principle

> Differentiate the output structure to match platform expectations while keeping the persona's voice consistent. The delivery mechanism adapts; the person does not.
